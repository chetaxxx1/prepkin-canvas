# Prepkin Canvas

register: product

## What it is

A free Chrome extension plus an iPhone companion app for students aged 16 to 22 who
live in Canvas LMS. The extension reads a student's own Canvas (courses, assignments,
submissions, grades) with the session already in the browser, quiets the Canvas page
(the "Receipt" skin: subtract and fix, every change listed and one click from coming
back), and shows the student's day in a popup and an on-page buddy panel. Finishing
real work, verified by Canvas, earns coins in the app; a small mascot, Sprout, lives
there. No accounts. Nothing is ever taken away.

## Users

High-school and early-college students, on a laptop, usually late, usually tired, with
one tab of Canvas open and a due date in the next 24 hours. They use extensions
subtractively ("remove X") and love the ones that feel like their own space. Their
school's IT department is the second user: it can block an extension district-wide in
an afternoon if it breaks quizzes, hides a submit button, or reads what it should not.

## Brand and tone

Warm, plain, quiet. A calm desk lamp next to a school page, not a dashboard. Copy is
read by a 16-year-old with a bio quiz on Friday: no exclamation marks, no engineering
words (never "sync", "bridge", "selector"), no scolding. Urgency is amber and reads
"still counts". Every number shown can only go up.

## Strategic principles

- The page belongs to the school. The skin is imposed, so it stays quiet; personality
  lives on the surfaces the student chose to open (popup, panel, app).
- Worst case is Canvas, unchanged. A missed hook leaves a plain page, never a broken one.
- Reads only the Canvas you connect. Sends your coursework list and course grades to
  your own phone, and makes no other request. No web fonts, no CDN, no analytics.
- Coins pay for finishing, never for grades. No meters, streaks, countdowns, padlocks,
  or `?` tiles. Accessibility (dark, contrast, motion) is never behind coins.
- The popup is the funnel: the one screen worth opening, and the one worth a screenshot.

## Anti-references

BetterCanvas / BetterCampus: settings-first popups full of toggles and theme grids,
hotlinked Pinterest images, a $19/month paywall, selectors on InstUI hashes that break
every deploy, a dark mode that once hid the submit button. Also: any "at risk" label,
any red, any hero-metric dashboard, any character forced onto a school page.
