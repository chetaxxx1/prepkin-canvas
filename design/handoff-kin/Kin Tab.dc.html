The rule this follows
Anything whose meaning is the movement gets removed (confetti, the reroll stagger in 1g, the care-row squash in 1a — which becomes a 160ms face cross-fade plus the toast). High school students are a population where Reduce Motion is genuinely common, and both of these screens exist purely to feel good. Treated as a design problem rather than a switch: decide per element whether the motion carries the meaning, then keep the composition identical either way.

Two rules that keep it low-chroma
Plate and badge text is always ink #2E2822, never white — all five tiers are light enough to carry it, so the type never changes weight or colour across the ramp. Legendary's coin disc inverts to #FFF3D6 on #A8761D because a #FFC24B disc on a #FFC24B badge would vanish; that inversion is the only per-tier exception in the whole system.
Unowned field, not grey
Owned cards are card white #FFFFFF; unowned cards are cream #FBF7F0 — one step off the background instead of a desaturation. The kin's own colours are never touched, which is what makes 1c readable without a single lock.
