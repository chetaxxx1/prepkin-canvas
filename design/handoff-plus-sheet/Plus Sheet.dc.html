1Plus sheet — three ways to put eight perks on one screen

The sheet in the repo is already the second draft, not the plum template: PlusSheet.swift has the kin hero, the free line first, five drawn perk tiles, the compare table, the gift timeline and the 3.1.2 block. So these three are arguments about what is still wrong with it. Three things, measured off the code. One: five perk tiles of 78×62 art beside two lines of copy is 440pt of list, and with the hero, the free line, two stacked price cards and five text rows under the button, Get Plus sits about 900pt down — the ask is below two screens of reading. Two: every card on the sheet carries shadow(radius:10, y:6), which is the thing Theme.swift took out of CardStyle on purpose — "a page of eight shadowed cards has no hierarchy left to spend". All three options drop it; the sheet itself is the only thing that floats. Three: PlusCompare.Mark.no renders an xmark in the Free column, and the brief's own words for that are "a cross is a padlock wearing a different hat" — so in all three the table only compares rows that have a value in both columns, and the Plus-only goods stay on the offer screen as things. One correction to carry forward from the Calendar handoff: Theme.muted is 3.46:1 on white, not 4.5 — I had that wrong. Captions here are #7B6C63 (5.0:1); muted stays for labels 19pt and up.
