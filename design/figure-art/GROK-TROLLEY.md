# Grok prompt: the one scene still missing

Three independent reviewers compared scene art against plain object art on the same
lessons. Only three figures earn a full scene: **spotlight**, **cave** and **trolley**.
Spotlight and cave are done. This is the last one.

Run it exactly as before: upload `design/figure-art/preview/scene-cave.png` in Grok's
edit mode, then paste the block below. Save the result as
`design/art-src/figures/trolley.png`.

```
Keep this exact drawing style, line weight, colours and flat background. Replace the whole scene with: A tram running down a track that forks ahead, five people standing on the straight branch and one person standing on the side branch, a person waiting at the switch lever beside the fork, a low footbridge crossing the track further back with one person standing on it. Drawn as a flat, wordless vector illustration for a learning app: big simple shapes, each filled with one flat colour from coral red #FF6F61, lavender #C3B2F0, sky blue #9BC8F2, and every shape traced with the same thick, even dark brown (#2E2622) outline. No shading, no gradients, no texture, no writing of any kind. The whole background is one plain solid pale sky blue (#E6F0FB). Empty sky across the whole top and a bare strip of ground along the bottom. Landscape 4:3.
```

Why this one is worth it: the lesson turns on a spatial fact, that the lever and the
footbridge are the same arithmetic but not the same act. Chips alone cannot say that.
