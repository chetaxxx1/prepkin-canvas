# Handoff: Home — the header band

Design date 2026-09-13 · brief `03-home-header-band/BRIEF.md` (Claude Design pack 2026-09-12) · target `ios/Sources/HomeView.swift`

Drawn in Claude Code's design canvas after Claude Design ran out of credits. Same rules as every
handoff: reuse the named components, the copy is `HomeCopy`, nothing is invented that the app
could not already know. Canvas: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/b8360640-b843-4adf-aa9d-d0e001b40306

## Overview

Home is tank → band → list. The band is pinned and carries three things: the kin's stage
(`StarPips` + words), the goals headline with the `Edit` pill, and one quiet line. This handoff
changes one thing: **the level plate goes.** The pips and the words stay, as an *eyebrow* above the
headline, in muted ink, on the bare floor colour.

| | Today | This handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Level | full-width tinted plate, 17pt pips, 39pt tall | eyebrow line, 13pt pips, 16pt tall, no plate |
| Gap to headline | 12 | 5 |
| Band height, quiet line present | ~123pt | ~93pt |
| Band height, no quiet line | ~103pt | ~73pt |

Thirty points come back to the list on every day. On the four-task evening the Tomorrow line now
clears the tab bar (artboard `A · Eyebrow` vs `Today`).

**Why the plate goes.** A full-width plate reads as a control or a meter. This one was neither: it
carried three pips and two words and could not be tapped. Every comparable puts a plain headline
row straight under its scene — Finch's "8 goals left for today!" row sits on the bare teal
([99094ecd](https://mobbin.com/screens/99094ecd-8045-4de7-a865-13ea77b3a9f0)); Finch only draws a
plate when there is a job inside it (the adventure bar with its countdown,
[7c5b20e8](https://mobbin.com/screens/7c5b20e8-19e5-42c2-a800-65f3bef5b958)). Headspace puts a
small muted line *above* the headline ("Good Evening, Alex" over "Start your day",
[1cba404f](https://mobbin.com/screens/1cba404f-413c-4a8b-be36-d791e9ca4fed)) — that is the eyebrow
pattern copied here: kin's stage → today's job → where the list came from.

**The grade calculator is not drawn.** It left Home on 2026-09-12 (Kin Card and Calendar's export
menu; `GradeCalcView.doors`), so the brief's first problem is already solved and the band gets no
fourth element. The tank stays 336pt (council, 2026-09-12); no height artboard.

## The band, top to bottom

Padding stays `.horizontal 18 / .top 12 / .bottom 10`. Outer `VStack(alignment: .leading, spacing: 5)`.

1. **Eyebrow** — `HStack(spacing: 7)`: `StarPips(level:, size: 13, spacing: 4)` then the level words
   in `Theme.font(12.5, .black)`, colour **`Theme.mutedInk`** (#5F534A, 7.4:1 on the floor). Words
   are `HomeCopy.levelLabel` unchanged ("Fully grown" / "Ready for the next star" / "40 to the
   next"). `fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)`. One accessibility element:
   "3 stars. Fully grown". No background, no padding of its own.
2. **Goals row** — unchanged: headline `Theme.font(16, .black)` ink, `Spacer`, the 30pt `Edit` pill
   (44pt tap).
3. **Quiet line** — unchanged: `HomeCopy.underGoals`, `Theme.font(12.5, .bold)`, `Theme.muted`.
   Absent when nil. The inner `VStack(spacing: 4)` around 2 + 3 stays.

Delete: the `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 18)` ink-8% plate, its `padding(.horizontal 14, .vertical 11)`,
the 17pt/5 pips, and the 12pt spacing that separated the plate from the goals row. Keep the
comment block that explains why there is no bar; shorten it to say the plate went too and why.

## Answers the brief asked for

- **Do the level band and a grade-calculator entrance share a row?** No entrance exists on Home
  any more. The eyebrow shares its row with nothing.
- **What happens at XL when the words run long?** Nothing shares a row, so the only long string
  is the eyebrow's words beside three fixed 13pt pips. At 1.4× "Ready for the next star" is
  ~200pt wide and fits beside the pips with 100pt to spare; if a future string wraps, it wraps
  under itself and the pips stay centred on the first line — acceptable, no special case.
- **Tank height.** Not drawn; decided 2026-09-12.

## Rules kept

No meters, no bar, no streaks, no countdowns, no red, no exclamation marks. One coral button per
screen (none added). Every string on one line is under 40 characters. `StarPips` is reused at a size
it already supports (its default is 13). Light only. The page background stays the tank floor
ramp; no card, seam or shadow under the tank.
