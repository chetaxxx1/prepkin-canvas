What this adds to the spec's eight perks. Add these rows to PLUS-SPEC section 2 with the same columns, then build in the order in the next block.
9. The season. Finch's event, ours: a three-week "season" tied to the term (Midterms, Finals), a daily ladder where finishing anything real that day claims the day. The Free column claims a small item each day. The Plus column claims a costume piece too, and Plus is guaranteed the full costume by the last day. Missed days never reset anything; a claim is a claim. The items are the 14 costumes and their pieces that already exist, so no new art is owed for the first season. Rule check: new items, so nothing moves behind money; every number only goes up; no streak because a gap costs nothing. Needs signature 12: "Seasons hand out items on a Free and a Plus track."
10. The compare table. Free vs Plus, checks and crosses, Structured's and Forest's shape, as the second screen of the sheet behind "See everything". The first screen stays the kin and five perks with pictures of the goods, Imprint's way.
11. "Support our mission." One line on the sheet, last: "Plus keeps Canvas, coins and every lesson free for everyone else." Finch and Duolingo both say it and reviewers forgive them for being cosmetic because of it.
12. Refer a friend, later. BetterCampus and Forest both do it. It needs App Store offer codes wired to the friend code, so it is a 1.1 item; write the row, do not build it.
Do not add: a lifetime plan (MARKETING-PLAN rejected it and the spec agrees), a coin boost, ads removal (there are no ads), a family plan, any AI feature that needs an account.
