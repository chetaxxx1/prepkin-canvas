# Closing the gaps on BetterCampus — planned 2026-09-18

Follows the element-by-element compare George asked for on 2026-09-18 (chat). Six gaps, one
person, ~17 hours. The Chrome Web Store listing is George's and is not in here.

The compare, in one line each. We win dark mode (20 pages A vs their dashboard), safety (off on
quizzes, sends nothing), calm (no toasts, no red pills, no streaks, no AI). We lose on a student
making it *theirs* (their own wallpaper is the #4 liked thing), on a course list you can reach
from anywhere with finished courses out of the way (#6), on GPA across terms (#5), on the recap
being seen (ours waits for December), and on reach (2M users, us 0).

## 0. Calls made, and why

| # | Question | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | A theme editor, or a photo? | **A photo.** One tile, "Your picture": drop a photo, it becomes a look. No colour pickers. | The editor is the thing students hate on theirs (18 people, 90 votes: it lost their CSS, refused their contrast). Image themes are what they love. The rail and accent are read off the photo, so the look holds together with nothing to set. |
| Q2 | Where does the photo live? | **This browser only.** `chrome.storage.local.ownArt`, shrunk in the page to ≤1920 wide, ≤1.5 MB, JPEG. Never in the bridge payload, never to the phone. | House rule: the skin sends nothing. The course pictures already work this way. |
| Q3 | Their sidebar course list? | **No sidebar swap.** Finished courses fold under the cards on the Courses tab and under the rows on /grades; the live classes are rows in Sprout's help panel on every page. | The sidebar swap puts their brand on the page (a top complaint). A fold is the same "hide past courses" with nothing to hide: it is closed by default. |
| Q4 | Past courses: fetch or infer? | **Fetch.** One more call per sync: `courses?enrollment_state=completed&include[]=total_scores&include[]=term`. Kept in `lastPayload.pastCourses`, apart from live courses, so nothing downstream sees a finished class as work. | The sandbox now has a Spring 2026 term with two concluded, letter-graded courses (seeded 2026-09-18) so this is tested against the real thing, not the fake alone. |
| Q5 | GPA: whose scale? | **The school's letter when Canvas has one, else the usual cutoffs. Said as an estimate, per term, plus overall.** Unweighted by credits: Canvas does not give credits. | Their GPA is a server page with transcript import behind an account. Ours is one line under the grades the laptop already holds. |
| Q6 | The recap before December? | **A "So far this term" card**, the same shape, four weeks in and eight things handed in, once a month, put away in one click. | The recap is the one thing on theirs anyone called amazing. Ours exists and no one will see it until the term ends. |
| Q7 | Mount time on a slow laptop? | **Measure it.** A CDP CPU throttle (4×) on the sandbox, four pages, skin on vs off, a number in the test output and a ceiling. | B8 on 09-14 was "9–20 s under load, flaky, needs a number". Still no number. |

Not doing: a theme marketplace, community themes, uploads of any kind, streaks, credits or
transcript import, calendar sync, six LMSs.

## 2. The steps

### 1. Your picture (5 h)

- `themes.js`: one more look, `own` — `{ id: 'own', name: 'Your picture', vibe: 'a photo of yours,
  under the paper', paper: { light: 'newsprint', dark: 'carbon' }, free: true, price: 0, art: 'own',
  wash: { light: 0.82, dark: 0.7 } }`. `artFor(theme, available, toURL, ownArt)` answers
  `{ wallpaper: ownArt.src, thumb: ownArt.thumb, cards: [] }` for it, or `null` with no photo.
- `themes.js`: `paletteFrom(pixels)` (pure, tested): the photo's mean colour, darkened to L≈0.22,
  is the rail; the most saturated hue bucket at L 0.42 (light) / 0.78 (dark) is the accent. The
  worn look is `{ ...LOOKS_BY_ID.own, ...ownArt.palette }` so `themeStyle` and `tileTokens` need
  no new branch.
- `content.js`: `readWallpaper(file)` beside `readPicture`: steps `[1920, .82] [1600, .78] [1280, .74]`,
  cap 1.5 MB, a 480×270 cover thumb, the palette. `wornLook()` replaces the three
  `LOOKS_BY_ID[wallet.wearing] ?? classic` reads; with `own` worn and no photo it is classic.
  `mount()` reads `ownArt`; `plannerLooks()` hands it to the tab.
- `boot.js`: reads `ownArt` with the rest, so the first paint already has the wallpaper.
- `planner.js`: the `own` tile leads "Yours". No photo: the scene is the paper with a dashed
  "Add a photo" — the tile is a `<label>` over a file input. With a photo: the tile is the photo
  under its wash like any image look; a small × in the corner removes it; the pictures block gains
  a "Your wallpaper · Change" line while it is worn.
- `receipt.js`: row `own-wall` (added) — "Your own picture, under the paper", on when the photo is
  worn; Put back = the paper row already covers it, so this row is `when`-gated with no hook.
  Cap 26 → 27 (a wallpaper on every page is a change to every page).
- Tests: unit `paletteFrom` (a red photo gives a red accent and a dark rail; grey gives the paper's
  own marks), `artFor('own')`, `skinClasses` with `own` worn ± photo. Receipt R37: on the Looks tab
  `setInputFiles` a 1600×900 JPEG → `html.pk-art`, `--pk-wallpaper` begins `url("data:image/jpeg`,
  the rail takes the photo's colour, reload keeps it, × returns to Classic, and R18 still finds
  nothing of ours on a quiz.

### 2. Finished courses (4 h)

- `canvas.js`: `mapPastCourses(rawCompleted, rawActive, colors, { now })` (pure): every completed
  enrolment plus any active one `isCurrentTerm` rejects for a passed term end. Row:
  `{ id, name, code, score, grade, term, termEndsAt, past: true }`. TA and observer rows out, as
  in `mapCourses`. Sorted newest term first.
- `background.js` `readSchool`: the one extra `getPaged`; `pastCourses` on the result and in
  `lastPayload`; **not** in the bridge push (`courses` stays as it is).
- `planner.js`: `plPastFold(courses, { heading })` — one line "Finished · 2 courses" with a
  chevron, closed; open: rows tile · name · term · letter or per cent, each a link to the course.
  Mounted after `DashboardCard_Container` on the Courses tab (`renderPastFold()` beside
  `renderPlanner`), and under the rows on /grades. Open state lives in the tab, not storage.
- `receipt.js`: row `past-fold` (added) — "Finished courses, folded under your cards", detect
  `pastFold`. Cap 27 → 28.
- `fake-canvas.js`: `world.pastCourses`, served on `enrollment_state=completed`; the default world
  gets two.
- Tests: unit `mapPastCourses` (a completed course, an active one whose term ended, a TA row
  dropped, sort). Receipt R38: fold present and closed on the dashboard, opens to two names with
  letters, links right, the Planner tab does not show it, the receipt lists it.

### 3. GPA so far (3 h)

- `canvas.js`: `letterPoints(letter)` (`A`→4.0 … `F`→0, tolerant of `A−`, `A-`, `A+`), and
  `gpaReport(courses, pastCourses, levels)` (pure): per course, the letter's points when Canvas gave
  one, else the cutoff points from the score; Honors +0.5 / AP +1.0 as today; grouped by term
  (`term` for past, "This term" for live); `{ overall, weighted, terms: [{ name, gpa, n }], letters:
  n, cutoffs: n }` or `null`. Replaces content.js `gpa()`.
- `planner.js` `plGrades`: the foot becomes a small block: the number large, "GPA so far · an
  estimate", one chip per term. Under it one line: "From your school's letters where Canvas has
  them, the usual cutoffs where it does not. Your registrar's number wins." Same block on /grades
  and on the Planner tab: one function, one fact.
- Tests: unit `gpaReport` (letters win over scores, A− reads, a past term groups, TA out, nothing
  graded → null, Honors bump). Receipt R31 grows: /grades shows the block with two terms.

### 4. So far this term (2 h)

### 6. Mount time under throttle (2 h)

  `on ≤ off × 1.5 + 1000 ms` and print the table. `npm run test:mount`.

## 3. Rules touched

- **Sends nothing.** The photo is a data URL in this browser; `pastCourses` never leaves the laptop
  either (not in the bridge push).
- **One place per fact.** GPA is one block used twice; the finished list is one fold used twice.
- **Never red.** Nothing new is red; the accent from a red photo is the photo's, not a warning.
- **Never a property on a button.** The own tile is a `<label>`; its input is `sr-only`.
- **The receipt cap** rises 26 → 28, both rows a change a student sees and can put back.
- **Estimates say so.** The GPA block says "an estimate" in the block, not a tooltip.

## 4. Risks

- A school CSP that bars `data:` images: the photo does not paint and the paper shows; same as the
  course pictures. Said in the tile's small print.
- `chrome.storage.local` has 10 MB without `unlimitedStorage`: photo ≤1.5 MB + eight course
  pictures ≤1.6 MB + the payload. Fine; the cap is what keeps it so.
- Letters differ by school (`A−`, `A-`, `HD`, `P`). Unknown letters fall to the score; a course with
  neither is left out and the block says how many it counted.
- Schools with forty finished courses: the fold lists them all; the GPA counts them all.

## 5. Built (2026-09-18, the same day)

All six steps landed on main (3a1ab864, 3ca1fafe, 604c95f2, 9989678c, and the mount selector
fix). Unit 148, receipt 48, floor 15/15 on dashboard · planner · /grades in light, dark, art.

What differed from the plan:
- The recap and the "So far" card had nothing to count: handed-in work leaves the task list
  three days after it is in (`DAYS_AFTER_SUBMIT`). Both now count from the marks (`mapGraded`
  keeps `dueAt` and `submittedAt`, sixty rows a course). The end-of-term recap was hollow
  before this too.
- Real Canvas keeps an empty `#dashboard-planner` with `display:none` on a Card View dashboard.
  The 09-17 List View detector read it as List View and folded every card-view dashboard (the
  Courses tab gone). `listViewShows()` now needs a node Canvas is showing; the fake's Card View
  carries the same hidden nodes so R35 and R38 hold the line.
- The help panel's class list is asserted through CDP's accessibility tree: the panel is a
  closed shadow root and this Playwright has no `page.accessibility`.
- The new InstUI Files page has no `.ef-*` nodes; the mount test waits for its search button.

Mount time, CPU 4×, median of three, 1280 wide, Deep Sea worn (the sandbox through the tunnel):

| Page | Plain Canvas | Skin on | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| dashboard | 6749 ms | 5001 ms | 0.74× |
| modules | 9667 ms | 5960 ms | 0.62× |
| files | 10332 ms | 11702 ms | 1.13× |
| page | 5142 ms | 5024 ms | 0.98× |

The skin does not slow a slow laptop; on the dashboard and modules it mounts sooner, because
Canvas's To Do and the module bands are folded before they draw. Files is the one page where
the skin costs, about a second at 4×.
