# BetterCampus & Canvas Extension Landscape — Public Research, September 2026

Research date: September 14, 2026. Every fact below has a source URL. Reddit was deliberately excluded (a separate sweep covered it already). Anything I could not confirm is labeled **UNVERIFIED** — I did not guess or fill gaps.

---

## Key findings (read this first)

1. **BetterCampus has about 2,000,000 Chrome users as of Sept 14, 2026**, up from roughly 1,000,000 in May 2026 — it roughly doubled in about four months. (Chrome Web Store, live fetch vs. chrome-stats.com's cached May snapshot.)
2. **It used to be a free theming tool called BetterCanvas. It's now a paid productivity suite called BetterCampus.** Free tier is thin; Pro is $19/month or $119/year. The name changed because of "copyright concerns" from Canvas LMS's owner, Instructure — per BetterCampus's own account.
3. **The same company then sent a cease-and-desist to a former contributor** who forked the old open-source code into a free tool called CanvasRefined, over reused code and the CanvasRefined name. Right-to-repair YouTuber Louis Rossmann publicized it; the company backed off. This is a one-sided account (the fork's own telling) — flagged as such below.
4. **There's an active user backlash.** BetterCampus's own public feedback board has multiple 2026 posts begging the company to undo the redesign and un-paywall themes ("BRING BACK THE OLD BETTER CAMPUS," "I'm so sad with the new update," "Just change it back").
5. **BetterCampus added a Duolingo/Finch-style gamified mascot: "Campy."** Students dress Campy, decorate a "Cabin," and earn XP and coins. Confirmed directly from their help center.
6. **Their public changelog is stale.** The newest dated entry is June 30, 2026, but the live Chrome version is 9.8.11 (Sept 14, 2026) — a jump of at least one full major version (a user complaint confirms "Version 9.3.0" existed by Aug 3, 2026) with no public release notes for any of it.
7. **The Firefox add-on is effectively abandoned**: last updated October 21, 2024 — before the rebrand even happened — versus near-daily point releases on Chrome. Firefox has ~33,000 users vs. Chrome's ~2,000,000.
8. **No official X/Twitter, Instagram, or YouTube channel was found.** Their real-time community is a Discord (~7,900 members) plus organic/fan TikTok and Lemon8 content.
9. **The competitor field is one giant extension (BetterCampus) and a long tail of tiny single-feature tools** (dark mode, GPA calculators — most under 5,000 users), plus two differently-positioned rivals: Tasks for Canvas (Coursicle, ~1,000,000 users, deliberately does not touch Canvas's code, markets itself as the "safe" choice) and Canvas Ninja (paid, quiz-focused, markets itself as blocking Canvas's quiz-monitoring).
10. **Instructure (Canvas's maker) shipped its own customizable learner dashboard in Q2 2026** and is replacing its free "Free-for-Teacher" tier with "Canvas Lite" (announced Sept 9, launches Sept 30, 2026) — timing that lines up with a massive Canvas data breach in May 2026 that was traced to Free-for-Teacher accounts specifically.
11. **No 2026-dated school IT notice, block, or warning naming BetterCampus was found.** The closest confirmed examples are from September 2025.
12. Popular student themes lean Studio Ghibli, pastel/pink, dark academia, and anime — plus, oddly, a whole "Lebron James" meme theme category with real traction.

Their own FAQ page, in their words:

## 6. Competitors

### BetterCampus, for comparison
Chrome: 2,000,000 users · 4.7★ (4.4K ratings) · v9.8.11, updated Sept 14, 2026 · Free + $19/mo or $119/yr Pro. Headline features: customizable dark mode, custom dashboards/themes, smarter to-do list, GPA calculator, notes/grade widgets. ([listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bettercampus-prev-betterc/cndibmoanboadcifjkjbdpjgfedanolh))

### Direct, named competitors

**Tasks for Canvas (by Coursicle)** — the one asked for by name.
- Chrome: [listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tasks-for-canvas-%E2%80%93-now-su/kabafodfnabokkkddjbnkgbcbmipdlmb) — **1,000,000 users**, **4.6★ (701 ratings)**, v1.6.4, updated **Sept 11, 2026**. Free.
- 5 headline features (from its own listing): (1) sidebar to-do list for Canvas **and now Blackboard/D2L Brightspace**; (2) color-coded progress rings per course; (3) streaks for submitting before the due date; (4) Gradescope sync; (5) card/list/recent-activity views plus a 24-hour clock option.
- Positioning, per Coursicle's own blog: they deliberately **do not modify Canvas's code at all** — framed as the safety-first alternative to extensions like BetterCampus, which they say "can interfere with core functionality, like not allowing you to turn in assignments," and note "many universities have blocked it due to these technical issues." ([coursicle.com/blog/better-canvas](https://www.coursicle.com/blog/better-canvas/))
- Note: there's also an **unofficial fork**, "Tasks for Canvas (Grey-Out Edition)," 5.0★, that adds visual graying-out of done/inactive tasks — third-party, not Coursicle's.

**CanvasRefined** — the free fork.
- Chrome: [listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/canvas-refined/ihienfbdfdamhmhhiokjnjmpjgbenedg) — **1,000 users**, **4.7★ (14 ratings)**, v7.0.0, updated **Sept 3, 2026**. Free, FOSS.
- Self-description: **"A FOSS (No payment and Open Source) fork of BetterCanvas from before they changed it."** Currently public beta.
- 5 features: dark mode + themes, grade analytics with history/trend charts, custom card CSS + background images, GPA presets and what-if calculations, and global search across modules/assignments.
- **Origin story** (per the fork developer's own account in the project's `theStory.md` — one-sided, not independently corroborated beyond the Rossmann mention): BetterCanvas grew to 2M users, then the team behind it changed the license on the old open-source repo and relaunched it as the paid BetterCampus. The developer says BetterCampus's legal team told them their MIT-licensed code couldn't be republished on the Chrome Web Store and "started talking about their lawyers and billable hours." The developer contacted FULU (Louis Rossmann's Free/Libre software advocacy org); Rossmann made a community YouTube post supporting the fork, and the legal pressure reportedly stopped. Source: [github.com/GuySandler/CanvasRefined/blob/main/theStory.md](https://github.com/GuySandler/CanvasRefined/blob/main/theStory.md).

**Canvas Ninja** — not in the original name list, but surfaced repeatedly as a direct competitor and worth flagging.
- Positioning: quiz-workflow tool, not a dashboard/theme tool. Headline claims: **"Privacy Guard" that blocks Canvas's quiz-tab-switch tracking**, AI-assisted quiz answers without tab-switching, and auto-fill across quiz attempts. States it "never asks for your Canvas login" and runs client-side.
- Price: **$16.99/month or $67.99 lifetime**, with a free trial.
- Its own comparison page calls itself "complementary" to BetterCampus, not a direct alternative. No user/rating numbers were shown on that page. Source: [canvasninja.app/vs/canvas-ninja-vs-bettercampus](https://www.canvasninja.app/vs/canvas-ninja-vs-bettercampus/). **This is the one product in this whole list whose core pitch (defeating Canvas's quiz-monitoring) schools would likely find most objectionable** — worth knowing for competitive/policy framing even though it's a smaller player.

**Important adjacent context, clearly a separate thing from BetterCampus:** in May 2026, **Instructure's Canvas platform itself** (not the BetterCampus extension) suffered a serious breach. Worth including because it's exactly the kind of event that would make a 2026 school IT department warier of *any* third-party Canvas tool, extensions included:
- Attacker group: **ShinyHunters**. Unauthorized access began **April 25, 2026**, detected **April 29**; on **May 7** they defaced Canvas's login page with a ransom note, timed to disrupt final exams.
- Scope: **8,809 institutions**, roughly **275 million user records**, about **3.65 TB** of data — names, emails, student IDs, and private student-teacher messages (passwords/financial/government-ID data reportedly not exposed).
- Root cause: attackers got in via **"Free-for-Teacher" accounts**, which don't carry enterprise-grade security controls — directly relevant to Instructure's own Canvas Lite replacement plan (Section 6).
- Resolution: Instructure says it reached an agreement with the attacker resulting in data destruction (an unconfirmed ~$10M payment has been reported); a class-action suit was filed May 13, 2026.
- Sources: [Wikipedia, "2026 Canvas data breach"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Canvas_data_breach); [U.S. Dept. of Education / FSA Partners security alert, updated May 29, 2026](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2026-05-12/technology-security-alert-ongoing-cybersecurity-incident-involving-canvas-learning-management-system-updated-may-29-2026); [NPR coverage](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/08/nx-s1-5815956/canvas-data-breach-school-finals).

**To be explicit: this breach was of Canvas/Instructure's own systems, not of the BetterCampus extension.** No evidence found connecting BetterCampus to the breach in either direction.
