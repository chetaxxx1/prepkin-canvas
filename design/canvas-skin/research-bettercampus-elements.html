What their theme actually is, in one line: five colours (background, surface, sidebar, links, text), one font, and a folder of card images. Everything else is a toggle that applies to every theme.

Ranked: what to build next

  1. Theme the nav rail. Colour or gradient per theme, wallpaper crop for image themes. Every Canvas tab stays, in the same place. This one change makes a screenshot read as "themed" the way theirs does.
  2. Grade pill on the course card. Top-left of the banner, from the same score the panel shows. Off when the school hides grades.
  3. Pick a banner per course. Three-dot menu on the card, choose one of the theme's banners. Six banners for Graffiti and the next flagship themes.
  4. Surface + links colours per theme. Two more fields in themes.js. Body ink stays computed for contrast.
  5. Course nickname. "BIO 101" instead of the registrar's title. Local, shown on cards and in the panel.
  6. Card roundness and spacing. Two toggles next to Compact pages.

What we keep doing differently, on purpose

  1. No font swap. Their serif look is pretty in a screenshot and slower to read for two hours.
  2. Canvas's nav stays Canvas's. We colour it, we never replace it, so nothing a teacher points to is missing.
  3. No URLs, no Pinterest links, no daily images. Art ships inside the extension. Zero requests leave the laptop.
  4. Course colour survives as the strip. Students learn "red is Physics" and we do not take that away.
