## Mechanics worth keeping whatever the look

- **`railShows()`** (`content.js`) is the one predicate for "the rail is on this page";
  the To Do fold (`detect.todoFold`) uses it, so no rail means no fold.
- **The fold** is receipt key `todo-fold`, kind taken. Show toggles `pk-todo-open`
  on `<html>`, which `applySkin`'s class sweep must keep (same as `pk-show`).
- **Sprout is two hosts.** `#prepkin-buddy` (panel, redraws with innerHTML) and
  `#prepkin-sprout` (the iframe; a moved or rebuilt iframe reloads). Never put the
  frame inside anything that re-renders.
- **Driving him:** `spriteSend({do:'play', emote:'wave'})` → postMessage → `sprout/bridge.js`
  → `window.RiverSprite`. Emotes: wave, curious, cheer, bounce, dance, sleep, surprise,
  pout; `signature`. He says `ready` once; earlier asks queue.
- **Which kin:** `wallet.kin` from `push_state` (`BridgeKin` in iOS `CanvasSync.swift`,
  built from `activeChibi` in `GameState.bridgeState`). `kinLook()` maps old species ids
  onto the six coats. No phone → stage-two mint.
- **Placement:** `spritePlace()` measures on every page pass. Tank: `.pk-w-tank` band
  108 px at the top of `#pk-week`, frame absolute over it, radius 30, panel host
  `data-side="tank"` opens to his left (`host.left = band.left − 372`). No week card →
  foot of the nav rail (radius 26/17). No rail → bottom right.
- **Refresh the bundle** with `extension/sprout/sync.sh` after any Sprout build lands in
  `ios/SproutWeb`. Manifest already has `sprout/*` and `'wasm-unsafe-eval'`.
- **Never** a selector with `css-`, never a property on a button, never red. R18 reads
  every `skin.css` selector off disk and asserts none matches a quiz being taken.
