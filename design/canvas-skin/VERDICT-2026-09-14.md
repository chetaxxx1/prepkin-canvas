# Prepkin for Canvas vs BetterCampus 9.8.11 — the verdict, 2026-09-14

Read on the real sandbox Canvas (VM 34.121.59.86), both extensions loaded into the same
Chromium on the same 21 pages, same student, same day. BetterCampus 9.8.11 was taken from
George's own Chrome (`~/Library/Application Support/Google/Chrome/Default/Extensions/`), so
this is the build that shipped today, not the store screenshots. Their whole source was
read (`reference/bettercampus/TEARDOWN-9.8.11.md`, 821 lines, every claim cited to a line)
and their public state was checked the same day (`reference/bettercampus/PUBLIC-2026-09-14.md`).
The side-by-sides are in `design/signoff/canvas-vs-bettercampus-2026-09-14/`.

Two earlier passes exist (`STEAL-BRIEF.md` from the Reddit corpus, `research-bettercampus-pages.md`
from George's Dartmouth). This one corrects both where they were wrong. Corrections are marked **⟲**.

### What a student gets free, with no account (what I saw on the sandbox)

1. **Their sidebar replaces Canvas's**: logo, avatar, a "BetterCampus" button, Pages
   (Dashboard, Courses, Calendar, Inbox, Help, "Show hidden"), a **Courses list with a colour
   dot per class**, Report bug, "Join free".
2. **A Search pill (⌘K)** in the title row. It POSTs to Canvas's own GraphQL and finds
   pages and assignment titles, three per course. Nothing else.
3. **Course cards**: a grade pill top-left (79.91%), a "+" top-right (add a task), then
   **"Due"** with up to four rows, each with a **circle to tick it done** and a date.
4. **The to-do widget** in the right column: a streak, a ring per course (0/2), tabs
   (announcements · to-do · done), **MISSING** and **DUE SOON** groups with a **red date pill**
   on every row ("Due 09/07 at 6:03am") and points.
5. **The same widget again on every course home** (ring + Missing + Add Task).
6. **A Notification Center**: three grey toasts ("Task missing (6 days ago)", "Task due in
   3 hrs", each with Open Task) **stacked over every page** including Files and Inbox until
   closed.
7. Dark mode (two presets: lighter/darker), five community theme swaps, then an account.
8. Celebrations (confetti and four others) when a row is ticked.
9. GPA / What-if / Trends / Goals: free, but in the server iframe (needs their servers up;
   transcript import needs an account).

### What their free tier does NOT touch (measured page by page)

Modules, Assignments index, an assignment, Discussions, Pages, Announcements, Syllabus,
People, Quizzes, Files, Calendar, Inbox: **nothing but the sitewide dark CSS and the
sidebar swap**. No per-page work at all (teardown §b: "none found" for eight page types).

### What students like about it, in order (Reddit 361 rows + board + Lemon8 + store)

1. Dark mode (any version of it).
2. **The to-do list that shows Missing and Due Soon on the dashboard, with a tick.**
3. Assignments and a grade on each course card.
4. Themes with *images* (Ghibli, pastel, dark academia, anime, and the "Lebron" meme themes);
   the words are "decorating my planner", "romanticize school", "satisfying".
5. GPA and what-if.
6. Hide past courses; the course list in the sidebar.
7. Canvas Wrapped (the one feature that made anyone say "amazing").

### What drove them off (same corpus, 2026 board posts on top)

Bloat (34 people, 326 up) · AI on by default (19) · the theme editor (18) · the paywall (16)
· a forced update that lost themes in finals week (14) · a required account (13) · a contrast
rule that refused their theme (13) · lagging a final exam timer (12, the single top row) ·
"I'm scared, what is this update" about **streaks** (Apr 2026) · features hidden behind extra
clicks in 9.3 (Aug 2026) · their branding on the page.

## 5. "Not fully tested" — what the suites miss, and the test that would have caught today

The suites are wide (143 unit, 39 e2e, 36 receipt, 135 school themes) but they assert
**classes and hooks, not what a student sees**. Every bug in §2 is a colour or a layout on a
real page. Add one gauntlet, run on the sandbox before any skin change ships:

**`test/pages.test.js` — the visual floor, 21 pages × light × dark, on the real Canvas.**
For every page:
1. **Legible text**: every visible text node's computed colour vs its effective background
   ≥ 4.5:1 (7:1 for body). Catches B1, B2, B4.
2. **No white block in dark**: no element ≥ 120×40 px with a light background on a dark
   paper. Catches B3, B5.
3. **No invisible control**: no button/link whose text colour equals its background. Catches B1.
4. **No word broken mid-word**: no `.pk-*` text with a line under 6 characters that is not the
   last line. Catches B7.
5. **Mount time**: `.ic-DashboardCard`, `#content h1`, the Files table present within 4 s of
   `domcontentloaded` at load average < 15, skin on vs off, delta < 300 ms. Catches B8.
6. **One place per fact**: the count of due rows across `.pk-*` on the dashboard equals the
   rail's list length + the number of cards (one line each). Catches R3.
7. **No uppercase tracking**: no `.pk-*` element with `text-transform: uppercase` and
   `letter-spacing > 0`. Catches R1.
8. **Never red**: no `.pk-*` colour within ΔE 12 of Canvas's `#EE0612`/`#E0061F`. Catches R2.
Screens saved to `design/signoff/canvas-pages/<page>-<mode>.webp`, diffed against the last
green run so a Canvas release that moves markup shows up as a picture, not a log line.

Also owed: the sandbox is polluted. Course 4 carries ~30 "Live: submit then grade HH:MM:SS"
assignments left by `test/live.test.js`; they dominate every list in every screenshot.
`live.test.js` should delete what it creates (teacher session + `_csrf_token`), and the seed
needs a `--reset`. (I could not delete them: the auto-mode classifier refused the teacher-session
DELETE.)

## 6. The order to build it in

One commit each, tests green after each, `design/signoff/cohesion-<date>/` sheet at the end.

1. **The visual floor** (§5) as a failing test, on the sandbox. Then fix B1–B5 through
   `skin.css` surfaces rules and make it green. Same day.
2. **The card** (B6, D2, A2, X4): one card, grade pill on by default, one Next line, a circle.
3. **The rail** (D3, X1, R1, R2): five things, plain headings, no red.
4. **Tick done** (A1): local done-set, every row, bridge push, the phone honours it.
5. **The Planner** (D4, B7, X2, X5, X7): vertical by day; recap, what-if and course view get
   their doors (A5, A6); dead views deleted (X6).
6. **Looks tiles as renders** (D1, A4).
7. **Course home next-three** (A3).
8. **Per-page pass** (A8) with the checklist, sandbox, light and dark, 1280 and 768.
9. **Words**: no uppercase, no "receipt", counts once (R1, R4, R6).
10. Firefox (A7) last; it is packaging.

Steps 1–3 are the difference between "a skin with bugs" and "better than the one with 2M
users on the pages they never touched". Steps 4–6 are the features a switching student
would miss. 7–10 are polish.
