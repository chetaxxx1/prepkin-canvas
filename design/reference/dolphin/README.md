# Dolphin SAT — 18 frames

Pulled from `~/Downloads/dolphin recording.MP4` (5m25s screen recording, 2026-08-12).
Mobbin does not index Dolphin; these are the only frames of it we have.

**This app is not on Mobbin and its social layer is thinner than its marketing.** There
is no friends list, no feed, and no Dolphin Party in this recording — Party shipped in
v1.1.4, after this was taken. What Dolphin actually has is a **league**, a **streak**, and
a **pet**. Two of those three we are refusing.

## The league — `01`–`07`. This is the pattern we are refusing.
- `01-my-league-empty` — Bronze League before you have earned anything: `-- Rank`,
  `0 Weekly XP`, `0d 0h Resets In`. Four league pills, three of them greyed.
- `02-my-league-bronze` — the earned state: **#29 Rank · 22 Weekly XP · 4d 8h Resets In**,
  "Top 5 advance to the next league".
- `03-league-promotion-zone` — a green **▲ PROMOTION ZONE** divider above rank 6.
- `05-league-demotion-zone-you-29th` — a red **▼ DEMOTION ZONE** divider, and *you*
  sitting at #29 inside it, highlighted yellow. This is the exact screen we will not
  build. It tells a student their week of work has moved them into the losing half.
- `07-global-leaderboard-top100` — global rank #3,582 against strangers with 4,054 XP.

## The streak and the calendar — `09`–`11`
`10-progress-streak-calendar` is the honest one: "1 Day streak · 22 XP", "Longest streak
0 days", a month grid of practiced days, and "213 days to go". Look at how *thin* that
streak reads on day one. Ours can only go up, so this is the anti-reference.

## The profile — `13`–`15`
`15-profile-league-history` is worth studying: Bronze "Highest League Achieved", Peak
Global Rank, **Longest Streak: 0**, Total XP 22. A profile that is mostly a record of
where you ranked. Ours should be a record of what you did.

## The pet — `16`–`20`. The part closest to us, and the part Dolphin does least with.
- `16-connect-desktop-hatch-pip` — **"Connect to desktop to hatch Pip"**, `dolphin.study/activate`,
  a 6-character code, "Expires in about 10 min". Nearly identical to our own extension
  pairing flow. Note it *gates the pet behind the desktop pairing* — we deliberately do
  the opposite; our app works fully without Canvas connected.
- `17`/`18-pip-quests` — "Today's quests 0/3, 265 XP left". Quests pay XP for answering
  questions **correctly** ("Answer 5 questions correctly +95"). That violates our economy
  rule. We pay for finishing, never for correct.
- `19-pip-looks-tier-ladder` — 6 locked skins earned by league tier (Bronze Reef, Silver
  Current, Gold Harbor, Diamond Wave, Emerald Mythic, Obsidian Abyss).
- `20-pip-looks-color-lab` — 6 recolours (Classic Blue, Coral Pink, Sunbeam Yellow, Kelp
  Green, Royal Purple, Midnight Black), 1 of 12 unlocked.

Pip is a flat blue dolphin in a box with an XP bar under it. It never reacts, never
appears anywhere else in the app, and is not social at all. That gap is our opening.
