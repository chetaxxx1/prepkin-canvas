# Reference frames — Friends design pass (Mobbin + a Dolphin screen recording, 2026-08-31)

62 PNGs, 390-wide phone frames with a Mobbin footer band. The prompt in
`design/CLAUDE-DESIGN-PROMPT-FRIENDS.md` refers to these folders by name.

## `finch/` — 15 frames. The closest fit to what we're building.
Tree Town in six states (`01`–`08`), including **`05-tree-town-empty-slots`**, which is
the nearest thing to our empty state: one friend, and the rest of the tree is `???`
signposts. `09-friend-profile-actions` is the friend card: Share Goal / Send Good Vibes /
Send Gift. `11`, `13`, `14` are the Good Vibes picker — 12–15 named cards in a grid
(Hello, Encouragement, Strength, Calm, Water, Thoughts, High Five, Stretch Break,
Birdhouse Love, Kudos, Sweet Dreams, Hugs, Gratitude), some locked behind Plus. That
grid is the direct model for our six vibe cards. `12` is Goal Buddy.

## `instagram/` — 11 frames.
`01`–`03` stories tray. `06`–`11` the viewer: segmented progress bar, the reaction bar,
and "Reaction sent". Note `07` — tapping the reply field opens a **keyboard**. We are
deliberately cutting that half.

## `together/` — 9 frames. Forest's Plant Together is the mechanic to copy.
`01` Create Room / Join Room next to a solo timer. `02` a room waiting on friends, with a
share-able **room code**. `04`/`05` join-by-code, including the error state. `06` invite
picker. `07` Forest's Deep Focus Ranking with Friends / Global tabs. `08` Runbuds shared
challenge. `09` Slack huddle, for how a live multi-person room reads.

## `leaderboard/` — 5 frames.
**`01-mimo-weekly-league-REFUSE`** is the pattern we are refusing: Wooden League, a
6d 22h countdown, and locked tiers above you. Keep it in the set so the refusal is
concrete. `04-deezer-challenge-a-friend` is a good result screen. `05-yubo-party-starting`
is the closest thing on Mobbin to Dolphin Party.

## `friendcode/` — 4 frames.
Numo, Tempo, Abode, Lapse — four takes on "here is your code, copy or share it".
Abode's is the best: the code big and coloured, with a QR and a line saying *don't share
this publicly*.

## `dolphin/` — 18 frames, cut from George's own screen recording. See its own README.
Not on Mobbin; pulled from `~/Downloads/dolphin recording.MP4`. **Dolphin has no friends
list, no feed, and no Party in this build** — its social layer is a league, a streak, and
a pet. `05-league-demotion-zone-you-29th` is the single screen we are most explicitly
refusing. `16-connect-desktop-hatch-pip` is its pairing-code flow, near-identical to ours.

## `imprint/` — from the Learn pass. Not part of this.
