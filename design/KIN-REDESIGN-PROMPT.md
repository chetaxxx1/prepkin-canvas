What to copy, and from where.

A. The pet page itself: Finch's "Lee" tab and home. The bird stands in its own house with a speech bubble, and the whole tab is the house, not a list. One band of chrome, the rest is the room. https://mobbin.com/screens/18d270f7-7eb4-448f-a928-174b4a7a7aa3 and https://mobbin.com/screens/d8ce4c68-ad5c-4400-a957-10a4d8befa2f. Ours: the tank fills the top of the tab, the kin in whatever it is wearing, the name plate and stars over the water, the bubble. Everything else opens from doors at the bottom of the tank, not from cards below it.

B. The doors: Finch's Bag. One sheet with four big tiles: Mail, Outfits, Furniture, plus two "?" tiles for things not yet reached. https://mobbin.com/screens/0f312de8-00a7-4902-9e42-630a60c7006f. Ours is a row of four doors under the tank, always all four, never a "?": Wardrobe, Decorate, Collection, Card. Each opens an editor or a sheet. The care buttons stay where they are, inside the scene, at the bottom edge of the water, the way Abode puts feed / clean / play inside the pet's scene: https://mobbin.com/screens/df4dc946-060d-45cd-b5da-9c35cfbdee68. Take the placement, never the heart and mood meters above it.

C. The editor shape: Instagram's avatar editor. Live preview on the top half, a bottom sheet with a tab strip (Style / Hair / …), sub-tabs under it, a 3-column grid with the price or "Free" under each tile, one Done button. https://mobbin.com/screens/e9d99b09-8296-4bb7-a36c-b5e76271e1b9 and https://mobbin.com/screens/387fd81d-fbe7-4c04-8885-dbd1403e105f. Finch's closet is the same shape with a silhouette icon per category and a NONE tile first: https://mobbin.com/screens/3fba181d-6016-4ac6-a28d-bf43f367ab4a. Ours: Wardrobe opens this editor with the tank as the live preview, tabs Costume / Coat / Scene, the NONE tile first, owned tiles tappable, unowned at full colour with the coin price (never dimmed, never a lock), a Save look row that writes to Saved looks. The Wardrobe card and the Saved looks card on the tab go away; they live in the editor.

D. Decorate: Replika's room and Tolan's planet. Replika: the room at the top, tabs Lounge / Study / Reflect / Decorate, a grid of props with a "Purchased" badge. https://mobbin.com/screens/31058bb0-c060-435d-b030-caaae13e3c78. Tolan: tap a slot on the planet, a ring shows where the thing will stand, pick from a tray below. https://mobbin.com/screens/1483da96-7cb5-46f9-b33f-095364d075c7. Ours: two prop slots per tank, left and right, inside the prop window the plates already keep. Tap a slot, the ring appears, pick a prop from the tray. Props are the composited pieces from design/tanks/compose_tank.py, so a prop is a PNG placed at a slot, not a new painting. If the prop pipeline is not in the app yet, build the editor against the two props each shipped tank already has and leave the tray with those.

E. The card: Finch's pet profile. Name, pronouns, "3 adventures", age in days, friendship level "Buddies", the human's name, the friend code, three little growth stats, a share button top right. https://mobbin.com/screens/b2af9c62-89dd-42c0-8704-94fa5e5483ba. Ours: the Card door opens the adoption certificate that already exists in AdoptionFlow, kept current: name, "Day 12 together", stars, the friendship stage (F), the friend code, three only-up stats (tasks finished together, lengths swum, lessons read), and Share. The share image is the kin on its tank plate at story size, the free marketing the launch plan wants. The Together strip on the tab goes away; it lives here.

F. The friendship ladder: Finch's friendship level. "You and Lee are Buddies. Increase your friendship level by consistently checking in, chatting and petting Lee." https://mobbin.com/screens/b2af9c62-89dd-42c0-8704-94fa5e5483ba (the sheet). Ours: five named stages that only go up, earned by days together and care taps, never lost, never a bar: Just met · Tankmates · Buddies · Besties · Old friends. The stage sits under the name plate on the tab as a word, and the card explains it in one line. No XP number, no progress bar; a stage changes on a quiet toast.

G. Firsts: Finch's quests list, without the hints. "Collect 5 pieces of clothing 3/5", "Obtain 5 decorations 1/5", "Evolve Lee to a Toddler". https://mobbin.com/screens/2180f7a4-b1a4-4615-8e1d-4d97d458de9b. Ours: a short "Firsts" list on the card, each a thing that happened once and stays: first costume, first scene, first friend, first shift, three stars. Done ones show the date. Undone ones show the words, never a "?", never a count.

H. A tankmate, later: Finch's micropet egg hatches after N goal completions. https://mobbin.com/screens/08078a81-0ffb-4dab-b3da-b3d810bf56ac. Ours would be a small second creature in the tank (a snail, a shrimp) that arrives after seven finished Canvas tasks. Needs art and a decision; write it as a row in KIN-PLAN.md and do not build it.

What is deliberately NOT copied: hunger, happiness or energy meters (Abode, Pou, BitePal); "?" tiles anywhere (Finch's Bag, Duolingo ABC's cards); XP numbers and skill bars (Ahead, Life Reset); a text chat with the pet (Abode, Tolan, Replika); personality quizzes; day-night as a purchase.
