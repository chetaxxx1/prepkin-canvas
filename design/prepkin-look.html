different apps. One look.

The pages don't disagree about taste. They disagree about rules. Each screen was designed on its own day, so each one invented its own header, its own card, its own icon. Below: what is actually breaking, the three rules that fix it, and three screens rebuilt on them.

Five tabs, five places the eye has to re-learn where it is. That is the whole feeling of "no uniformity" — it starts in the first 90 points of every screen.

The direction

Three sources, three jobs, no overlap

Blending all three everywhere is the trap. Each one gets a territory and stays in it. That is what makes the mix read as deliberate instead of busy.

Finch

owns objects
  • Every icon is a full-colour object. No mono glyphs, no SF Symbols in content — chrome only (chevron, ✕, +).
  • Every object sits in a 52pt squircle, radius 16, tinted with its own hue at ~12%.
  • Six tints, fixed. An icon does not get to pick a new colour.

Imprint

owns the page
  • Near-white page, white cards, hairline not shadow. Shadows are for things that float: tab bar, sheets.
  • Same header on all six tabs: title left at 26/900, coin chip right, 18pt gutter.
  • Air is a feature. One idea per band, 14pt between bands.

TikTok

owns one moment
  • Exactly one loud thing per screen. Never two.
  • The hero number gets the cyan/red split — the timer, a coin payout. Nothing else, ever.
  • Play lives in a near-black full-bleed band that runs off both edges. Energy is fenced, not sprinkled.
The rules

Five radii, six type steps, six tints

Small enough to hold in your head, which is the only test that matters. These go in Theme.swift and every screen is edited to use them.
