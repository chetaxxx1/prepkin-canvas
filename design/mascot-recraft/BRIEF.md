# Sprout for 19-24, both genders — change brief (2026-09-08)

Source: design/research-mascot-comparables.html (six ranked moves). Body stays. Face, palette and tone change.

| # | Change | Now | Proposed | Why |
|---|--------|-----|----------|-----|
| 1 | Eyes | big dots, r=63, close-set | 20% smaller, 15% wider apart, slight flat top (lid) | big low eyes = baby schema (Glocker 2009) |
| 2 | Blush | always on | off by default; a coat option | blush reads toddler and skews female (Finch ~75% women) |
| 3 | Resting mouth | U smile | flat line or 1px smirk; smile only on emotes | deadpan is the adult counterweight (Duolingo, Gudetama) |
| 4 | Coats | candy pastels | desaturate ~20%, darken ~10%: mint→sage, sky→slate, lilac→dusty, butter→mustard, coral→terracotta; tank darker | pastels read young and gendered |
| 5 | One edge | none | per kin: side-eye idle, a fin nick, a leaning sprout | Labubu / Kuromi rule: cute + one flaw holds 18-24 |
| 6 | Stance | squat | ~5% taller, fins a touch lower | less toddler, more "guy at the desk" |
| 7 | Gender balance | pink/blush/pastel defaults | neutral coats first in picker, humour + collect looks (ninja etc.) carry the rest | Duolingo 50/50, Habitica 51/49 via humour and loot |

Not changing: pear silhouette, sprout tuft, fins, tank, costume system, evo badges.
