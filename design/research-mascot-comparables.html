Who Sprout has to win over, and what they already buy

Comparables for 16 to 22 year olds: the companion apps they keep, the characters they spend on, and the games whose art they don't call childish. Every number links to its source. Marketing claims are flagged.

Verdict. Sprout's body is fine for university students. Limbless round blobs are the most age-neutral shape in the market: Squishmallows' fastest-growing buyers are adults, Among Us and Fall Guys are beans, Kirby is a ball. What dates him is the face, the coat and the voice. Big high eyes plus blush is the baby formula from the science. Candy pastels read toy aisle. Earnest cheer reads kids' app.

The products that hold 18 to 24 all pair a cute base with one adult counterweight: deadpan tone (Duolingo, Gudetama), an ugly edge (Labubu, Kuromi), restraint (Miffy, Notion), or a collecting economy (Pop Mart, Animal Crossing skins). Sprout needs one of those. He currently has none.

The science of "looks like a baby"

Lorenz's baby schema, tested with modern methods, says the same three things every time. Bigger eyes, a rounder face and a larger forehead raise cuteness ratings and the urge to look after the thing. That is exactly what you do not want for someone who is 20 and paying for SAT prep.

  • Glocker et al. 2009. Enlarging eyes, forehead and face roundness on infant photos raised cuteness and caretaking motivation, and lit up the brain's reward centre. Ethology
  • Borgi et al. 2014. The effect works on dogs and cats too, and viewers' gaze goes straight to the eyes. So the eyes are where age is read. Frontiers in Psychology
  • Nittono's two-layer model. "Cute" is an emotional response plus a social frame. Adults feel it for objects that are not babies. Kawaii is not the same as childish. The frame around it decides. East Asian Journal of Popular Culture

One correction to what I showed you earlier. In the baby schema, infants have eyes set below the head's midline under a big forehead. Adults' eyes sit at the midline. So the reliable lever is eye size and blush, not pushing the eyes down. On Sprout the pear is the whole body, so where "the midline" sits is a judgment call. Treat eye position as something to test, not a rule.

What this means for Sprout

Ranked by how much evidence sits behind each move. The first three are safe. The last three are bets.

  1. Change the voice before the art.Dry, short, a little deadpan, never a shame spiral. No exclamation points. Duolingo holds 60% under-30s on attitude, not softness. Focus Friend's whole pitch is "no judgement". Gudetama was designed for burnout.
  2. Eyes about 20% smaller, set a touch wider. Blush down to almost nothing.Keep the dot style. Every baby-schema study points at eye size. Labubu, Sonny Angel, Miffy and Notion's figures all have small simple eyes. Finch's big-eyed bird is the one comp that got called infantile.
  3. Sell looks and collect them. Stop calling stages "evolutions".Ninja is the right axis. Add blind-box style drops of coats and badges. Pop Mart, Sonny Angel, Animal Crossing and Fall Guys all turn cute into a collecting hobby. Collecting reframes a toy as a habit adults admit to.
  4. Mute the coats by about 20% and let tanks go darker.Sage, not mint. Ochre, not butter. One saturated accent per kin. Labubu, capybara, Hollow Knight and cozy games all sit in muted palettes. The counterexample is Duolingo, which stays bright because the tone carries it.
  5. Give him one edge.A side-eye idle. A flat mouth at rest. A weird trait that is only his. Kuromi's punk, Labubu's teeth, Pikmin's eeriness, Cult of the Lamb's horror. Each is a cute base with one thing that is not sweet. Sprout is all sweet.
  6. Keep the body exactly as it is.Do not add limbs or a face-and-body split. Limbless blobs are the most age-neutral shape in the data: Squishmallows' adult boom, Among Us, Fall Guys, Kirby. This is the one thing already right.

Gender note

The soft pastel companion register skews female: Finch is about 75% women, cozy games 45 to 55% female, Sonny Angel buyers 62% female. The comps that hold men at the same age do it with humour (Duolingo, near 50/50), loot and competition (Habitica, 51/49), or slapstick (Fall Guys). Moves 1, 3 and 5 are what keep the boys.

What I could not verify

  • Duolingo's "under 30" share and its post-campaign download lifts come from secondary write-ups, not a filing.
  • Fall Guys and Among Us age splits are from aggregator sites. Treat as directional.
  • No demographic data surfaced for Kirby, Pikmin, Miffy, Pusheen or the capybara trend. Their entries are qualitative.
