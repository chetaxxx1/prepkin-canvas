Four are shared with the vibe set so the vocabulary is one vocabulary — a student learns five shapes, not eleven. Warm is viewer-only: a heart is fine as a reaction to something that happened, and too loaded as a card you post to someone. One per friend per day, coral fill on the sent one, the rest inert at 35% until tomorrow.
Screen 8: ship the group total
Make 1o the default and the only thing on the tab root; keep 1p behind the second segment, one tap away.
Why the total wins
With five friends a ranking has exactly one winner and four people who lost to their friends — and the person at the bottom is usually the one having the worst week. The total is the only framing where a quiet week reads as fine. It also survives the rules: a sum can only go up, so there is nothing to defend and nothing that resets on Monday.
Why keep the board at all
Some students genuinely like the ladder, and hiding it entirely invites screenshots-of-screenshots comparison outside the app. One tap away, ranked on minutes rather than coins, with no zones and no decay, it is honest without being the frame everyone opens into. If usage says otherwise, the segment is cheap to remove.
The number I'd actually put on the tab root over time is together time — hours two or more of you were in a room at once. It is the only metric on this tab that measures the thing the tab exists for.
