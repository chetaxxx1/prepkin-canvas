What this course is

AP Physics C: Mechanics is calculus-based physics: kinematics, Newton's laws, work and energy, momentum, rotation, oscillation and gravitation. We move fast and we derive things. Bring the calculus.

How the grade works

  • Problem sets, one a week, due Friday at 8am. The lowest two are dropped.
  • Lab writeups, five over the term.
  • Two unit checks and one project proposal.

Late work loses 10% a day for three days, then it is a zero. Talk to me before the deadline, not after.

Office hours

Tuesday and Thursday, 3:30 to 4:30, room 214. Or write on Canvas and I answer within a day.

Texts

Halliday, Resnick and Krane, Physics, volume 1. The PDF of each week's slides is in Files under Lecture slides.
