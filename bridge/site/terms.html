The short version

Prepkin is a Chrome extension that restyles your own Canvas, and an iPhone app with a fish that grows when you finish work. Your Canvas account stays yours. Coins and looks are for fun only. Prepkin Plus is billed by Apple. You can turn the extension off or remove it at any time, and Canvas goes back to how it was.

What Prepkin is

Prepkin has two halves that work together:

  • A Chrome extension that changes how Canvas looks on your laptop, using the login you already have in your browser
  • An iPhone app where a fish grows as you finish schoolwork and other tasks on your day

Neither half replaces Canvas. They sit on top of the work you already have.

Who can use it

Prepkin is for people aged 13 and over. Your school's Canvas rules still apply while you use it. Prepkin is not made or endorsed by Instructure or by your school.

Your Canvas account

Your Canvas account stays yours. Prepkin never asks for your Canvas password. The extension uses the session already open in your browser. If you remove the extension or turn its Canvas look off, the page goes back to Canvas as it was.

Coins and looks

Coins, fish looks, and other in-app rewards have no cash value. They are not money, cannot be sold, and can change or go away as Prepkin changes.

Prepkin Plus

Prepkin Plus is an optional paid plan on the iPhone app. Apple handles the bill:

  • $3.99 a month, or $19.99 a year
  • Cancel any time in your Apple settings
  • When the plan ends, you keep everything you already had

Prices shown here are what the app offers today. Apple shows the current price before you buy.

No promises about school

Prepkin does not promise better grades, more finished work, or any school outcome. It is a tool for your day and how Canvas looks. How you use it is up to you and your school.

Changes and stopping the service

Prepkin can change or stop. When we can, we will give notice. Turning the extension off or removing it stops its changes on Canvas right away. Deleting the app stops the phone half.

Prepkin is responsible for running the extension and the app with care, and for keeping your coursework private as described in the privacy policy.

Prepkin is not responsible for:

  • Your grades, assignments, or anything Canvas or your school decides
  • Problems caused by your school network, device, or browser
  • Loss of coins, looks, or other in-app rewards if the service changes or ends
  • Anything you choose to do with your schoolwork after Prepkin shows it

If something goes wrong with Prepkin itself, the most you can ask for is a refund of what you paid Apple for Prepkin Plus for the period that did not work, where Apple's rules allow it. Prepkin is not liable for school results, lost time, or other indirect harm.
