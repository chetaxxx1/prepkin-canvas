Prepkin

Support

Prepkin is a Chrome extension that quiets your Canvas page, and an iPhone app where a fish grows as you finish work. The two halves can share your coursework list when you connect them, but each one also works on its own.

Email support@prepkin.com and we will help.

What to include

In your message, say which half you are writing about (iPhone or Chrome), your school's Canvas address, and what you expected to happen.
